Aldersgate College

Vision

A 21st Century university transforming a global network of learners for enlightenment, leaderrship and human service.

Mission

Aldersgate College was establish to develop the power of reason, to liberate people from ignorance, penury and hopelessness,and to empower them to commence their own journey for social and economic transformation and spiritual salvation.

Core Values

ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE- Train students to become academically and professionally competent in the fileds of arts and letters; natural and physical Sciences; Business; Management and Economics; Engineering and technology;Education; and the health Sciences.

SOCIAL RELEVANCE- Impress norms, values and behaviors that are socially relevant to the human development needs of ther majority and the less fortunate and at the same time responsive to the ideals of a democratic society.

SPIRITUAL NURTURANCE- Evolve and inculcate a spiritual knowingness founded on absolute biblical truth and concurrently promoting unity,moral uprightness and dedication to Christian Service.
